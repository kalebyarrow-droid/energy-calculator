Calculated bill

Estimated bill = usage × current provider rates + daily supply − export credit. Enter your actual billed amount to track differences. The billing-period savings figure is calculated against an optional comparison tariff, not an assumed saving.

Preliminary system sizing

Preliminary estimates use saved billing-period totals, not interval-meter data. Actual solar self-consumption and battery requirements depend on hourly loads, roof orientation, shading, export limits and installation quotes. Do not treat these estimates as installation specifications.

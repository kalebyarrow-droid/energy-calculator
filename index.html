Calculation notes

Solar generation is estimated from installed kW × average daily yield × monthly seasonal factors. Daytime solar first offsets daytime loads; surplus charges the battery and then exports. Batteries can charge from off-peak grid power, discharge into expensive periods, and lose energy through round-trip inefficiency. Battery capacity and solar output degrade each year. Investment opportunity cost uses the specified annual return. This is an indicative financial model, not an interval-meter simulation. Solar export earnings and grid-charged battery economics depend heavily on actual hourly consumption and export limits. Assumes no equipment replacements, financing, rebates, tax effects or maintenance costs. *25-year net = cumulative avoided electricity costs minus upfront system cost and investment opportunity cost (future value of initial capital). Electricity rates escalate annually.
